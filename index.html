Frequently Asked Questions

How do direct video downloads work?

When you enter a direct media link (e.g. .mp4, .webm), SnapStream streams the bytes through the client-side Fetch and Blob API directly to your device storage, bypassing third-party trackers.

What if a platform restricts downloading or uses DRM?

SnapStream strictly adheres to platform security policies and copyright standards. Protected media, DRM content, and paywalled streams cannot be extracted and will display an error notice.
